# Auth / No-login Blockers

No login, OAuth, credentials, or private workspace access was used.

## Blocked in no-login mode

- Exact DOM/CSS for authenticated Notion editor/workspace internals.
- Private workspace sidebar states, settings, billing, member management, and permission screens.
- Hover/focus/selected states that require a live account with editable pages/databases.
- Real user content, comments, and workspace-specific templates.

## Available substitutes

- Mobbin MCP public visual references saved under `evidence/mobbin/`.
- Screenshot-derived OCR/palette/layout facts under `components/extracted/from-mobbin-screenshots/`.
- Public no-login Notion homepage/pricing DOM/CDP evidence under `evidence/web/`.
