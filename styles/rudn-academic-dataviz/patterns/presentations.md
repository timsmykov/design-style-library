# Presentations

## Deck rhythm

Alternate explanation, worked example, task, checkpoint, and synthesis. Do not run more than three dense explanation slides without an action or visual reset.

## Title slide

- Small status/series label.
- Large two-part title with one blue phrase.
- One concise subtitle.
- Thin horizontal rule; no decorative footer or logo wall.

## Method slide

- State the method in plain language.
- Show 3-4 numbered steps on one baseline.
- Put the formula or key transformation at visual center.
- Add the teaching simplification in a lavender support band.

## Comparison slide

- Shared title and two columns.
- Highlight only one side with blue fill when the asymmetry matters.
- End with a neutral conclusion band that explains why both sides may be valid.

## Task slide

- Separate `what you have` from `what you must produce`.
- Show timebox in blue.
- Put fixed constraints and submission condition in support bands.

## Projection gate

- Title >= 28 pt, body >= 15 pt, sources >= 10 pt at 960 x 540.
- If a slide fails at room distance, split it. Do not solve density by shrinking type.
