# Digital surfaces

## Shell

Use a white canvas, a narrow content column or clear 12-column grid, black headings, and indigo controls. Electric blue remains the instructional highlight inside content.

## Components

- Buttons: indigo fill, white label, 8 px radius, no gradient.
- Links: indigo, underlined on hover/focus.
- Cards: white, 1 px gray border, 6 px radius, no shadow.
- Callouts: lavender fill, dark text, square/soft corners.
- Tables: strong header hierarchy, thin rules, sticky headers only when needed.

## Responsive behavior

- Collapse multi-column comparisons into a single ordered sequence.
- Preserve title -> evidence -> action reading order.
- Convert wide process strips into numbered vertical steps.
- Keep chart labels direct and visible; do not hide essential values behind hover.
