# Layout

## Presentation geometry

- Ratio: 16:9; source evidence is `960 x 540 pt`.
- Safe margin: 5-7% of width and 6-9% of height.
- Working grid: 12 columns with 20-28 px-equivalent gutters.
- Title zone: top 14-20% of the frame.
- Main content: one full-width idea, two balanced columns, or a three/four-step strip.
- Support band: bottom 12-18% for caveat, source, prompt, or takeaway.

## Archetypes

1. **Statement:** large title, one sentence, one rule.
2. **Comparison:** two columns with a clear shared axis and a concluding band.
3. **Process:** 3-4 numbered steps on a common baseline.
4. **Worksheet:** labeled cards or rows with explicit completion criteria.
5. **Evidence visual:** insight title, chart/table, annotation, source note.
6. **Checkpoint:** one dominant target card plus one recovery path.

## Alignment

- Left-align prose and titles.
- Center only formulas, oversized metrics, or a deliberate comparison symbol.
- Reuse exact left edges across title, content, and footer.
- Use rules to bind sequences; do not float equal-weight cards without a shared axis.

## Density

- Comfortable: one argument, 3-6 content units, 35-55% deliberate whitespace.
- Dense tables are allowed only when the task requires item-level lookup; increase visual scale for projected use.
