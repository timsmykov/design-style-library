# Hierarchy

## Type scale for 16:9 slides

- Display: 40-54 pt, Inter Bold, 1.0-1.08 line height.
- Slide title: 28-36 pt, Inter Bold, 1.08-1.15.
- Section title: 20-24 pt, Inter Bold.
- Body: 15-20 pt, Inter Regular, 1.25-1.4.
- Label: 10-13 pt, Inter Bold or Regular, often uppercase only for status.
- Source/caveat: 10-12 pt, never below projector legibility.
- Oversized numeral/symbol: 48-88 pt, Inter Light or Regular.

## Emphasis order

1. Meaningful title.
2. Blue keyword, number, or outcome.
3. Black body explanation.
4. Lavender support band.
5. Gray metadata.

Do not bold whole paragraphs. Do not color several unrelated phrases blue. Avoid all-caps except compact status labels such as `CHECKPOINT`, `VERBATIM`, or `FIXED`.
