# Anti-patterns

- Blue gradient backgrounds or gradient text.
- Full-frame brand color washes.
- Glass cards, glow, bevels, or deep shadows.
- Rounded-card grids where every item has equal emphasis.
- Stock photos of students, campuses, robots, or dashboards used as filler.
- Rainbow charts or default spreadsheet palettes.
- Tiny sources, legends, or table text.
- Formulas without a worked example and plain-language interpretation.
- Tables without a comparison task or highlighted finding.
- Decorative icons with mixed stroke weights or emoji styles.
- Several blue accents competing on one slide.
- Using `#4950BC` and `#2E6BFE` as two equal categorical colors; they have different roles.
- Invented RUDN logos, seals, ornamental patterns, or claims of official university compliance.
