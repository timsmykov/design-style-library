# Presentation Pattern

Translate the style into slides without copying brand assets.

## Slide surface

- Warm ivory background.
- Charcoal text.
- Clay accent used sparingly.
- Large whitespace.

## Typography

- Serif/editorial title for opening or conceptual slides.
- Sans-serif for labels, bullets, charts, UI annotations.
- Avoid dense slides; use one idea per slide.

## Composition

- Use editorial two-column layouts: argument on one side, figure/proof on the other.
- Prefer calm diagrams, document cards, and artifact panels over glossy hero images.
- Use black/charcoal section dividers or inverse cards for technical/developer moments.

## Visuals

- Human/symbolic, flat, cut-paper/editorial, or sparse information design.
- No robot/brain/neon AI clichés.
