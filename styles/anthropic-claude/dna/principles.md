# Principles

1. **Paper before chrome** — start from warm paper/ivory canvas and subtle surfaces, not glossy dashboard panels.
2. **Editorial hierarchy before decoration** — typography, whitespace, and section rhythm carry the design.
3. **Workbench over chat toy** — AI output should become useful document/code/file/artifact surfaces, not only message bubbles.
4. **One decision, one action** — keep CTAs sparse and obvious; secondary actions stay quiet.
5. **Show proof next to claim** — product cards pair concise copy with a concrete UI/code/document preview.
6. **Trust through transparency** — privacy notes, source/status/context, familiar auth, and recovery paths beat decorative trust badges.
7. **Clay accent is scarce** — use terracotta/clay for identity or primary execution moments only.
8. **Human, not robot** — brand visuals are human/editorial/symbolic, not robots, brains, lasers, or neon AI clichés.
