## Capsule

# Two-column comparison
## Structure
Shared question -> two aligned columns -> matched comparison rows -> conclusion band.
## Recipe
- Use identical row order on both sides.
- Apply blue fill to one side only when the contrast benefits from an anchor.
- Keep the conclusion in a lavender band across both columns.
- State why the difference matters; do not stop at listing attributes.
## Avoid
Unequal categories, mismatched baselines, or two bright colored panels competing for attention.
