## Capsule

# Course title slide
## Structure
Small series/status label -> large title with one blue phrase -> concise subtitle -> thin rule.
## Recipe
- Use the upper-left quadrant and keep at least half the canvas empty.
- Title 40-54 pt Inter Bold.
- Highlight the unit name or meaningful variable in `#2E6BFE`.
- Keep subtitle black and compact.
- No illustration unless it explains the course topic.
## Avoid
Centered title blocks, logos as wallpaper, gradients, dates and metadata competing with the title.
