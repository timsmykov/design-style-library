## Capsule

# Evidence and caveat band
## Structure
Optional compact icon/label -> one concise sentence -> optional source or method detail.
## Recipe
- Lavender `#DADBF0` fill with `#272525` text.
- Use 12-20 px equivalent padding and 4-6 px radius.
- Keep the band visually subordinate to the title and main content.
- Reserve for claims, caveats, prompts, and evidence; never as decoration.
## Avoid
Stacking multiple bands, embedding long paragraphs, or using low-contrast lavender text.
