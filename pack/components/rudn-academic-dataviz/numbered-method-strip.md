## Capsule

# Numbered method strip
## Structure
Common baseline -> 3-4 numbered zones -> bold action -> one-line explanation -> optional lavender caveat.
## Recipe
- Keep all steps at equal visual level unless one is the current step.
- Use thin black/gray rules, not separate floating cards.
- Number with compact labels or circles.
- Blue may highlight the active variable, deadline, or result.
## Avoid
Long paragraphs, decorative arrows, mixed icon sets, or more than four visible steps in one row.
