# Layout

## Brand / marketing pages

- Warm ivory full-page canvas.
- Minimal top nav aligned to a central max-width container.
- Large editorial whitespace.
- Two-column hero when visual identity matters: left copy/CTA, right large symbolic human/editorial image.
- Section transitions use generous vertical spacing and confident headings.

## Product cards

```text
Large rounded card
├── Left: title, short description, one CTA
└── Right: product proof visual, code snippet, chat/file preview, or artifact demo
```

- Light card = approachable end-user/conversational mode.
- Dark card = developer/API/code mode.
- Avoid dense multi-card grids until the primary story is established.

## App / workbench

- Compact left sidebar or rail.
- Subtle divider; no heavy chrome.
- Large calm canvas.
- Prompt/composer can sit bottom-centered as a command card.
- Artifact/file/code panels get first-class space when work output exists.

## Modal / onboarding

- Dim background shell.
- Centered warm off-white rounded modal.
- Preview/demo area above or beside explanation.
- Short copy, one CTA, optional small status pill.
