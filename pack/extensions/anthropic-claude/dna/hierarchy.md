# Information Hierarchy

## Default priority order

1. Human-readable task/result/section title.
2. Context or proof: product preview, artifact, source/file/code context, example prompt.
3. Primary action.
4. Secondary metadata: model, branch, source, privacy note, status, timestamp.
5. Tertiary controls: overflow, secondary links, dismiss, legal.

## Brand pages

- Headline and symbolic visual can share first-read priority.
- Body copy is short and calm.
- CTAs are compact but high contrast.

## Workbench pages

- Primary focus is the composer or current artifact.
- Sidebar history/navigation is secondary and muted.
- Context chips are visible but low-emphasis.

## Avoid

- Equal-weight button rows.
- Badge soup.
- Multiple cards competing as first read.
- Dense tiny tables without document-like framing.
- Hero gradients or decorative AI imagery above useful content.
