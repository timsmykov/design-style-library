# Voice

Claude-style copy is calm, human, and problem-solving oriented.

## Use

- Short, clear, human sentences.
- Phrases that frame the user as capable: problem solver, thinking partner, build together, tackle complexity.
- Transparent caveats when needed.
- Specific outcome language.

## Avoid

- Hype: revolutionary, 10x, magical, insane, dominate.
- Generic SaaS filler: streamline your workflow, unlock productivity, supercharge everything.
- Over-technical language in brand/onboarding moments.
- Long legal or trust explanations in the main hierarchy.

## Example tone

- “The AI for problem solvers.”
- “Tackle any big, bold, bewildering challenge.”
- “Create with Claude.”
- “Bring your knowledge.”
