# Forms and Onboarding Pattern

## Use for

Signup, login, verification, trial start, account setup, feature onboarding.

## Layout

- Calm two-column page when brand mood matters: form on one side, warm human/editorial visual on the other.
- Or centered modal/card when onboarding happens inside the app.
- Keep form fields minimal.

## Components

- Social auth can appear first as a light button.
- Email/action CTA uses dark filled button.
- OR separator is small and quiet.
- Privacy/legal copy is visible but tertiary.
- Header CTA may exist, but the form CTA owns the decision zone.

## Trust mechanics

- Familiar auth.
- Privacy link.
- Calm copy.
- No urgency pressure.
- No unnecessary data requests.

## Avoid

- Multi-field forms before value is clear.
- Loud trust badges.
- Aggressive conversion copy.
- Secondary actions competing with the main form action.
