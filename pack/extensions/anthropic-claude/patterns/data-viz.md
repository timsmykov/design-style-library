# Data Visualization Pattern

## Chart style

- Warm ivory canvas.
- Thin warm-gray gridlines.
- Charcoal labels.
- Clay accent for the primary series or important annotation.
- Muted neutrals for secondary series.

## Layout

- Chart should feel like an editorial figure with a clear title and explanatory caption.
- Put interpretation near the chart, not hidden in legend clutter.
- Prefer direct labels over dense legends when possible.

## Components

- Formula cards and annotations use subtle off-white/white surfaces.
- Use dark inverse panels only for code/data-console moments.

## Avoid

- Rainbow palettes.
- Neon highlights.
- Dense tiny labels.
- Decorative 3D charts.
