# Principles

## 1. Teach the reading order

The layout must reveal where to start, what to compare, and what to do next. Use alignment, numbering, and a single blue emphasis rather than ornamental arrows or animation.

## 2. Evidence is part of the composition

Source, period, unit, method, and caveat belong near the claim. Lavender bands are reserved for these support layers.

## 3. Energy comes from precision

Use bold titles, strong contrast, large numerals, and decisive whitespace. Do not add visual noise to make academic content feel modern.

## 4. Explain before displaying

A formula, chart, or table needs a plain-language interpretation. The viewer should know what the visual proves or what action it enables.

## 5. One chromatic protagonist

Electric blue is the instructional protagonist. Indigo is the quieter action/link role. Lavender is a support surface. Everything else remains neutral unless semantic status requires it.
