# Voice

## Character

Direct, academically responsible, encouraging, and operational. The tone says: here is the question, here is the method, now verify the evidence.

## Writing rules

- Use short declarative headings.
- Prefer verbs: compare, calculate, verify, explain, propose.
- State constraints explicitly and near the task.
- Separate fact, teaching simplification, hypothesis, and interpretation.
- Use examples with exact units and periods.
- End task frames with a visible definition of done or checkpoint.

## Avoid

- Corporate inspiration language.
- Generic AI hype.
- Overfriendly filler.
- Unqualified certainty.
- Long prose copied onto slides.
- Decorative English when the learning language is Russian; bilingual labels are allowed when they support source search or terminology.
