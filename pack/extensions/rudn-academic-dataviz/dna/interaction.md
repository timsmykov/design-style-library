# Interaction

## Controls

- Primary action: indigo `#4950BC` fill with white text.
- Instructional selection or active learning step: electric blue `#2E6BFE`.
- Links: indigo by default; electric blue inside a slide title or teaching annotation.
- Focus ring: 3 px pale blue plus a 1 px dark outline when needed.
- Radius: 6-10 px; pills only for compact tags or status.

## Motion

- 120-180 ms ease-out for hover/focus.
- 180-240 ms for panel reveal.
- No bounce, parallax, looping decoration, or chart animation that delays reading.
- In presentations, prefer progressive disclosure only when it follows the method step by step.

## States

- Success: check icon plus text; do not rely on green alone.
- Warning: pale warm band plus explicit label; use sparingly.
- Error: red accent plus recovery action.
- Disabled: visible neutral state with sufficient contrast; never merely reduce opacity below legibility.
