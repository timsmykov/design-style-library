# Evaluation rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 2. A passing artifact scores at least 13/16 with no zero in evidence, accessibility, or rendered quality.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Message | unclear/multiple | understandable | one decisive question or insight |
| Reading order | ambiguous | mostly clear | immediate and deliberate |
| Typography | inconsistent/tiny | usable | disciplined Inter hierarchy |
| Color roles | decorative/conflicting | mostly correct | semantic and restrained |
| Evidence | missing | partial | source, period, unit, method complete |
| Data visual quality | default/noisy | readable | insight-first and directly labeled |
| Accessibility | color-only/low contrast | minor gaps | robust contrast and redundant cues |
| Render quality | clipping/overflow | minor defects | clean at target size |

## Automatic reject

- Invented official RUDN branding.
- Unreadable projected text.
- Unsupported numerical claim.
- Rainbow or 3D chart that obscures comparison.
- Major clipping, overlap, or missing glyphs.
