# Failure modes

## Looks like generic corporate training

**Symptom:** rows of rounded cards, stock icons, motivational headings.

**Fix:** return to one learning question, thin rules, worksheet geometry, and one measurable checkpoint.

## Too much blue

**Symptom:** blue background, blue cards, blue headings, blue chart series.

**Fix:** restore white canvas and black hierarchy. Keep one blue protagonist and one lavender support layer.

## Blue and indigo compete

**Symptom:** `#2E6BFE` and `#4950BC` appear as equal categories.

**Fix:** electric blue is instructional/chart emphasis; indigo is link/button/control behavior.

## Academic but unreadable

**Symptom:** dense tables and long caveats at tiny size.

**Fix:** split the slide, move detail to a handout, or show only the rows required for the current task.

## Chart without an argument

**Symptom:** metric-name title and default legend.

**Fix:** state the finding in the title, directly label the focal mark, and add source/method.

## False brand authority

**Symptom:** invented crest, logo, pattern, or claim that these are official university brand standards.

**Fix:** describe the system as the RUDN course visual style derived from supplied course artifacts until official brand evidence is added.
