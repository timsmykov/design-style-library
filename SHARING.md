# Sharing and publication policy

## Visibility

This curated snapshot is intended for public GitHub visibility after the
security and personal-data checks recorded in `PUBLICATION_AUDIT.md`.

## Included

- style DNA, tokens, patterns, component capsules, evaluation rules, and tools;
- the self-contained `pack/` runtime distribution;
- generated style-component slices under `gbrain_export/components/`;
- reference assets already tracked in the curated snapshot, subject to their
  original owners' rights and the documented provenance records.
- locally archived Mobbin-derived screenshots and metadata; live Mobbin URLs
  are intentionally removed and no subscription is required at runtime.

## Excluded from the shareable snapshot

- all private and operational G-Brain exports outside `gbrain_export/components/`;
- local credentials, `.env` files, logs, caches, and browser profiles;
- raw HTML or authenticated DOM captures flagged by secret scanning because
  they contain embedded client API configuration.

## Licensing boundary

The VoltAgent `awesome-design-md` baseline remains available under its bundled
MIT license. Reference screenshots, captured pages, logos, and other third-party
assets remain the property of their respective owners. Unless a file states
otherwise, no additional open-source license is granted for the Hermes deep
extensions. Public visibility permits viewing and sharing the repository; it
does not transfer third-party rights or create a blanket reuse license.
